# My Plan

Branch: A
I will create a cheerful character with more than 100 visible
freckles made from small SVG circles. The freckles will be
arranged across both cheeks and the nose. The character will
have round eyes and a wide smile.
Branch: B
I will create a playful cat character using two distinct images:
a cat-ear image above the head and a bow-tie image below the
chin. I will draw both images myself and save them as SVG files.
Branch: C
I will create a calm character with a face shaded by a gradient
from warm orange at the top to cool purple at the bottom.
A cubic Bézier curve will form a gentle smile.

## Selection Criterion

I will choose the portrait whose expression and main visual
details are easiest to recognize at a small size.
